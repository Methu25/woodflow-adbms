# Business Context & System Requirements
**Organization:** Local Furniture Reselling & Custom Manufacturing Business  
**Location:** Kottawa, Sri Lanka  
**Current State:** Manual Excel spreadsheets for income, expenses, and informal record-keeping.

---

## 1. Business Overview
The business operates a hybrid model:
1. **Showroom Retail (Resale):** Purchasing finished, ready-made furniture items from suppliers and retailing them to walk-in/phone customers.
2. **Custom Manufacturing (Workshop):** Designing and manufacturing bespoke furniture pieces tailored to customer specifications (custom dimensions, wood types, finishes, fabrics, and upholstery).

---

## 2. Core Operational Modules Required

### A. Sales, Invoicing & Billing
- Quotations and estimates for custom inquiries.
- Invoicing for both ready-made stock and custom work.
- Multi-tier payment tracking: upfront advances, partial progress payments, and final balance settlements upon delivery.
- Printable PDF invoice generation.
- Automated customer delivery of receipts/invoices via WhatsApp.

### B. Income & Expense Management
- Categorized recording of operational overhead (rent, electricity, transport, machinery maintenance, tool replacements).
- Reconciliation of daily cash, bank transfers, and card receipts against bank deposits.
- Real-time financial reports: Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Operating Expenses, and Net Profit.

### C. Furniture Order & Production Tracking
- Work order creation linked to customer orders.
- Milestone tracking: *Design -> Carpentry / Cutting -> Assembly -> Cushioning / Upholstery -> Polishing / Painting -> Quality Inspection -> Ready for Delivery*.
- Delivery scheduling and logistics tracking.

### D. Inventory & Material Management
- **Finished Goods Stock:** Ready-made furniture units, cataloging serial/batch numbers, purchase costs, and retail selling prices.
- **Raw Materials Stock:** Timber boards/logs, upholstery fabrics, foam sheets, hardware (screws, hinges, handles), paints, varnishes, glues.
- Automated stock decrement upon sale or allocation to a production job.
- Re-order alerts for fast-depleting raw materials.

### E. Workforce Management, Payroll & Piecework Labor
- Core staff payroll: Monthly fixed salaries, attendance, statutory deductions, and advances.
- **Job-based Artisan Piecework:** Sub-contractors or carpenters paid per furniture item produced or per task completed.
- Dual-role workers: Handling employees who receive a baseline monthly retainer plus piecework bonuses without double counting or transaction overlap.

### F. Analytics & Business Intelligence Dashboard
- Real-time receivables dashboard (who owes what).
- Job progress board for workshop oversight.
- Material wastage and cost variance reports.
- Comprehensive monthly profit-and-loss reports replacing spreadsheet summaries.
